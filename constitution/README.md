# Second Edition constitutional changelog (21 September 2026)

The authoritative constitutional text is Appendix A of
[*Relational AI: Constitution, Architecture, and Empirical Evaluation of Relational Development*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6578778)
(SSRN, September 2026). This folder contains the constitutional changelog of the Second
Edition, [`constitutional-changelog-2026-09-21.md`](./constitutional-changelog-2026-09-21.md),
and this index.

## Two changelogs, and the earlier principles
- [`constitutional-changelog-2026-09-21.md`](./constitutional-changelog-2026-09-21.md) records
  constitutional revision: enacted texts, superseded texts (verbatim), genealogy, and
  interpretive notes. Its entries of 21 September 2026 are reproduced verbatim and without
  alteration. The file as a whole differs from the version completed on 21 September 2026 in
  one respect: a dated record-clarification entry, approved by the author, has been appended
  at its end.
- [`../CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md) is the repository's design-provenance record for
  implementation choices. It does not record constitutional amendments.
- [`../PRINCIPLES.md`](../PRINCIPLES.md) preserves the pre-September principles and is superseded.

## Records held by the author, not published here
The changelog refers to deliberation records, reviews, and the author's written decisions.
They are held separately by the author and are not published in this repository. These
records were examined in a consistency audit in September 2026; their full text is not
available to readers of this repository. They are:
- deliberation records for Articles 1, 7, 10 and 12 (20–21 September 2026);
- the author's written responses to the questions in those records (22 responses);
- the joint Article 1/Article 10 reconciliation and enactment review, the Article 7
  enactment review, the Article 12/General Clause 3 enactment review, the Article 5
  compatibility review, and the terminology review recommendations (21 September 2026);
- the author's written Article 12/General Clause 3 enactment confirmation;
- the author's written instruction opening the Article 5 review and stating the Article 3
  and Article 11 clarifications, and the Interpretive Clarifications document drafted from
  it at the author's direction;
- the author's written approval of the terminology harmonization.

The author's final approvals of the Article 1/Article 10 package, of Article 7, and of the
Article 5 amendment (including the change from "hold" to "govern") were given in
conversation messages. In the materials examined for that audit, their content is recorded
in the changelog entries for those events, but the original messages were not recovered.
The changelog records their content; it does not reproduce the messages.

## Reading the changelog's wording
Earlier changelog entries say records are "preserved in full as part of this entry." Those
records are not reproduced in the changelog and are not published here; see the
record-clarification entry at the end of the changelog.

SSRN v2 (Section V) describes the amendment program as "preserved there in full — deliberation
records, superseded texts, admitted overclaims." The superseded texts and admitted overclaims
can be read in the changelog in this folder. The deliberation records are held by the author,
and a reader of this repository cannot inspect them.
